## Hi, I'm Artem

I’m an 19-year-old passionate **Computer Science student** at the University of Łódź, specializing in **Game Development** and **Software Engineering**.

I love creating games and bringing ideas to life through code and art. With hands-on experience publishing indie games on **mobile and PC**, I’ve reached over **20,000 downloads** on Google Play and am always excited to take on new challenges.

###  Projects I'm Proud Of

**Cold Warms Us**  
A 2.5D top-down survival shooter featuring day/night cycles, crafting, inventory, and survival mechanics — developed solo over 10 months.  
[Check it out on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2797540/Cold_Warms_Us/?beta=0)

**Indie Mobile Games**
