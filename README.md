## Hi, I'm Artem

I’m an 20-year-old 3rd year **Computer Science student** at the University of Łódź, specializing in **Game Development** and **Software Engineering**.

With hands-on experience publishing indie games on **mobile and PC**, I’ve reached over **20,000 downloads** on Google Play and am always excited to take on new challenges.

---

###  Projects I'm Proud Of
**Later** 

Currently collaborating with two friends of mine on a social app named "Later", built with Flutter (frontend) and Firebase (backend). The platform’s core feature enables users to create and discover digital time capsules that are unlocked only when visiting their specific physical locations

**Cold Warms Us**  

A 2.5D top-down survival shooter featuring day/night cycles, crafting, inventory, and survival mechanics — developed solo over 10 months.  
[Check it out on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2797540/Cold_Warms_Us/?beta=0)
